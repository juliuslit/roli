# MenuStat: source and licence

- **What:** MenuStat, the NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene's database of nutrition and menu information from the largest US restaurant chains. Each item carries the chain, the item name, a food category, the serving size and unit, and nutrients per serving and per 100 g, with flags for kids' meals, limited-time offers, regional items and shareable items.
- **File:** `menustat_historical.csv`, 65,220 lines (65,219 items; 44,936 distinct `Menu_Item_ID`s), years 2017 and 2018, 96 chains. SHA-256 `85eb2b4737d3f1bb35f14a3dbc1b3ca460160e95c51b1b6497497809aa84e54a`.
- **Where from:** NYC Open Data, dataset "DOHMH MenuStat (Historical)", id `qgc5-ecnb`, published by the Department of Health and Mental Hygiene: https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Health/DOHMH-MenuStat-Historical-/qgc5-ecnb. Downloaded 2026-11-06 (EST) by `curl` from `https://data.cityofnewyork.us/api/views/qgc5-ecnb/rows.csv?accessType=DOWNLOAD`. The dataset's metadata (`meta.json`, saved beside it) states "Date Made Public: 5/10/2018", "Update Frequency: Historical data", rows last updated 2019-02-04.
- **menustat.org itself** (the spec's first-named source) refused every request from this machine on 2026-11-06 with Cloudflare's "Attention Required" page (HTTP 403), with and without a browser user agent. The NYC Open Data file is the Department's own publication of the same database, not a third-party copy, so it is used. It holds two years, not the 2012-2019 range the spec names; the live site may hold more.
- **Licence:** NYC Open Data states no dataset-level licence. The New York City Administrative Code, Title 23, Chapter 5 (the Open Data Law), § 23-502(d): "Such public data sets shall be made available without any registration requirement, license requirement or restrictions on their use provided that the department may require a third party providing to the public any public data set ... to explicitly identify the source and version of the public data set, and a description of any modifications made to such public data set." § 23-504(a): "Public data sets made available on the web portal are provided for informational purposes. The city does not warranty the completeness, accuracy, content or fitness for any particular purpose or use of any public data set." (Read 2026-11-06 at https://nycadmincode.readthedocs.io/t23/c05/index.html.) The portal's terms page (https://www.nyc.gov/html/data/terms.html) returned 404 on 2026-11-06.
- **How Roli uses it:** evidence for the plate-size table and the reference check only (SPEC_3_mvp.md 12.16.6-7). No MenuStat figure enters a total. The file is not modified; `roli_agent/scripts/build_cards.py` reads it and writes the summaries to `roli_agent/eval/card_evidence_<date>.md`.
