# USDA FNDDS 2021-2023: source

- **What:** USDA Food and Nutrient Database for Dietary Studies (FNDDS) 2021-2023, as published through FoodData Central as "Survey Foods (FNDDS)". 5,432 foods and beverages (4,827 foods, 605 beverages), each with 64 nutrients per 100 g (saturated fat and cholesterol among them), its WWEIA food category, portion weights (22,046 across the release) and the input foods USDA built it from.
- **File:** `surveyDownload.json` (66,294,426 bytes, dated 2024-10-25 inside the archive), one JSON object with a single key `SurveyFoods`.
- **Where from:** FoodData Central download page, https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/, item "Survey Foods (FNDDS)", release 10/2024, file `FoodData_Central_survey_food_json_2024-10-31.zip` (3,835,292 bytes). Downloaded 2026-11-08 by `curl` from `https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/fdc-datasets/FoodData_Central_survey_food_json_2024-10-31.zip`. SHA-256 of the zip: `dfb06ae7ddc397ccd570b91c14b75438ab2ba39f64f22d321f61d4a52a77f3eb`.
- **Licence:** USDA data; US federal government work, public domain (FoodData Central asks that it be cited: "U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service. FoodData Central, 2019. fdc.nal.usda.gov"). The nutrition service's saved USDA answers in `.cache/usda_store` come from the same release (`release.json`: Survey (FNDDS) 2024-10-31).
- **How roli2 uses it:** `roli2/data/sources.py` reads description, WWEIA category, nutrients 1258 (saturated fat), 1253 (cholesterol), 1008 (energy) per 100 g, and the portion list; a per-plate figure is formed only from a portion that is a whole dish, fillet or restaurant order. The file is not modified.
