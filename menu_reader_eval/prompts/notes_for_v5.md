# Notes for a future prompt v5 (not used this round)

Collected while scoring. Prompts v1, v2 and v4 stay unchanged for round one.

- **Rule 4 is ambiguous when the "options" are different dishes.** Rule 4 says options printed in smaller type under an item, without their own price, go into that item's description. On photo 21 the board prints "PIZZA BY THE SLICE 5" over "VEGETARIAN / COMBO / PEPPERONI": three different pizzas at one price. The key has three items; Haiku returned one item with has_choice yes and the three names in the description. Both readings follow the rules. A v5 should say which it wants, for example: "If the options are different dishes that a person orders by name (pizza flavours, sandwich fillings with their own names), list each as an item and repeat the shared price." (2026-10-29)

- **Rule 3 needs a worked example.** Models still list lines that are cut off mid-word by the photo edge (photo 17's left column, photo 13's right column), which now fails pass bar 2b. v5 should show one: a line such as "...occoli w/ Garlic Sauce" left out of `items` and mentioned in `problems` ("left column cut off: one or more lines unreadable"), so the model sees what "leave it out" looks like in the JSON. (2026-10-29)

Three additions after the Qwen3.6 batches (2026-10-30), each with the example a v5 should carry:

- **Headings and footnotes are never items.** Both open-weight prompts returned section headings and slogans as dishes: "SANDWICHES", "WRAPS", "TOASTIES", "EXTRAS", "REN'S BURGERS", "VEGETARIAN", "OUR HOMEMADE PIE", "OUR DELICIOUS HAMBURGER with MELTED CHEESE", and price tiers such as "2 FILLINGS". v5 should state the rule and show a negative example: a menu block headed "TOASTIES" with three lines under it produces three items whose `section` is "TOASTIES" and no item named "TOASTIES"; a line like "all served with chips" goes into the descriptions or `problems`, never into `items`.
- **Cut-off lines are left out and mentioned in `problems`.** Every model still listed the lines cut mid-word at the photo edge (photo 13's right column, photo 16 and 17's left column), which now fails pass bar 2b. Example for v5: the edge shows "...occoli w/ Garlic Sauce"; the correct answer has no such item, and `problems` says "left edge cut off: one or more Tofu & Vegetable lines unreadable".
- **Option lists that are different dishes are separate items sharing the printed price.** Rulings on photos 12 and 05: "Childs Half Sandwich 3.50 - ham / cheese / cheese & ham / cucumber & hummus" is four items at 3.50; "Jacket potatoes 7.95 - ham and cheese / beans and cheese / barbecue pulled pork / Mediterranean roast vegetables" is four items at 7.95; "Pizza by the slice 5 - vegetarian / combo / pepperoni" is three items at 5. The heading line itself is not an item. (Supersedes the open question in the first note above.)
