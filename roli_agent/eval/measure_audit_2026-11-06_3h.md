Shallots are the largest real gap: no USDA table has a whole shallot, so they fall under the near-zero rule. "2 cups unbleached all-purpose flour" is listed as a real gap, but USDA has a cup measure under "Wheat flour, white", a name the same-food test cannot see. The "record" column shows the before record: "4 whole cloves" matched peaches in 3G and matches ground cloves in 3H.
