## Findings by cause

**Roli cannot size the dish at all (the largest group).**
- "No portion rule" 108 dishes on 12 menus, sandwiches, burgers, wraps and tacos 30 on 5, sides 28 on 4, desserts 18 on 4, breakfast 14 on 2, children's 3 on 2. Desserts, sides and children's dishes are outside on purpose; the rest is the main gap.
- Of the 36 burger and sandwich dishes, 33 were excluded. Burgers read as sandwiches, as the watch list expected. Two steak or pork dishes with a bun got through as meat mains: Dwarf Grill's Deluxe Steak and Ren's Pulled Pork. Both stayed unranked because "Served on Large Toasted Bun" and "hamburger or brioche buns" have no weight.

**The 14-dish cap: 97 dishes on 4 menus.** Alex's Kitchen alone lost 79 (150 food dishes) and Pizzaface 14. It was never a problem on a short menu.

**One plate worked out as several whole recipes (recurs on 3 menus; this is what a tester would notice).** The key-ingredient method has no ceiling.
- Pizzaface: "Sundried tomato", a dip, shows 513-1076 g saturated fat and 2695-5615 mg cholesterol. It is impossible, and it ranks second. The dip reads as a tomato salad, whose rule wants 120-250 g of tomato, and the recipes have 13.5-120 g of sun-dried tomato, so a "plate" is up to 18.5 recipes of cream-cheese dip.
- The Greyhound: Crispy Squid & Prawns, 1.5-42 g: 6.9 recipes on one line's 17.5 g.
- Alex's Kitchen: Chicken Corn Soup reads as a meat main at 140-280 g of chicken: 3.3 recipes. Chicken Mushroom Soup shows 17-56 g.

**Pizzas.** The biggest single cause on pizza is a dough line with no USDA record ("store bought pizza dough", "pizza dough ball", "purchased fully baked thin pizza crust"), on 01, 02, 03 and 06. 8 of Pizzaface's 14 tried dishes found no recipe, because their names are invented (Vesuvius, Nadine, Nutsman, Johnno, Almonda, Fumo, Nutriel, Matto Vgn). Its Margherita is 6.4-68 g: one recipe at 67 g because a recipe with an unknown dough weight counts as a whole pizza. "Marinara sauce", a dip, reads as a pizza.

**Words the reader carries over.** On 04 the wine pairing went into the descriptions, so Carricante, Etna, Sicily, Schiava, Istria and Croatia '21 became menu-only ingredients and held up all 4 tried dishes. 04 ranks 0.
